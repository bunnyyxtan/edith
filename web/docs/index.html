Pay with context.

Edith gives an AI agent a way to ask for paid API data without giving it control of your wallet. It checks previous outcomes and your spending limits, asks you to approve the transfer, then records what the payment returned.

Who it is for

Edith is for people who let agents use APIs that charge per request. It is especially useful when the same agent may ask again later, after a paid response proved empty, failed, or overpriced.

The agent sends a URL to Edith. Edith can refuse it, return a free response, or ask the wallet owner to approve a quoted payment. An agent key cannot sign or approve a transfer.

Before you begin

You need a supported Sui wallet in your browser, such as Slush. The live app uses Sui Mainnet. The guided demo's first two steps each request a real 0.001 SUI payment plus network fees. You can read these guides and inspect the app without paying.

Payment control. Connecting a wallet and signing in do not move funds. Each paid request needs a separate wallet signature after you review its price and recipient.
